Results from a live Meta ads account

A clothing brand's Meta ads account, February to September 2026, from problem to result.

1

Client and context

A clothing brand's online store, run through Ad2ship. Channel: Meta ads only. My role: Performance Marketer, then Growth Manager. Period: Feb to Sep 2026. Spend: ₹68 lakh.

2

The problem

As spend scaled to ₹13.7 lakh in June, ROAS slid from 8.3x in April to 4.8x in August. Cost per order rose from ₹193 to ₹351.

3

My approach

  • Read the daily data to find where cost per order broke.
  • Rebuilt creative testing around AI-generated concepts and variations.
  • Planned influencer-led content and used creator videos as ads.
  • Cut weak ad sets and scaled the winners daily.
4

The solution

Two changes turned the account around.

AI creativesA repeatable pipeline of AI-generated concepts and variations, so fresh ads reached the account every week instead of waiting for fatigue to hit.
Influencer marketingA creator-led content plan, using influencer videos as ad creative to build trust with fashion buyers.
5

The result

August₹351cost per order
→
September₹221-37% cost per order
ROAS4.8x → 6.8xon 37% lower spend

What I learned: creative volume and creator trust protect ROAS better than budget alone.
